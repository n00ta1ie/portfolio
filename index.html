about

a mission to fight plastic pollution

operation ocean is my final major project: a 3d animated short that recruits kids as secret "ocean agents" on a mission to fight plastic pollution, paired with a craft tutorial showing how to turn a plastic bottle into a recycling-bank turtle.

the campaign also includes a full brand identity, a website and physical stickers and pins, all designed to make ocean conservation feel like a fun mission instead of homework.

the film

the animated short

a short 3d animation following a young ocean agent on their first mission: spotting plastic waste and turning it into something worth saving. paired with a step-by-step craft tutorial that turns an empty bottle into a turtle recycling bank.

craft tutorial

bottle to turtle

a step-by-step guide for turning an empty plastic bottle into a turtle recycling bank; the hands-on companion to the film.

campaign

brand identity

a logo, badge system and mark for "ocean agents", playful enough for kids to want to wear, sturdy enough to print on a real enamel pin.

supporting materials

stickers, pins & the website

physical takeaways and a digital home for the mission, extending the same world from screen to sticker sheet to browser.

university brief 2025—26 branding · graphic design · 3d · ui design
the brief brand & logo brochure website outdoor booth interior banner social
the brief

an unopened book, waiting to be read

a university brief to reimagine the brand and visitor experience for the marx memorial library: a real archive of marxism, socialism and the working-class movement in clerkenwell, london. after a site visit and research into the space, our team landed on a guiding idea: the library as an unopened book, full of stories most people walk past without ever knowing they're there.

i led the visual design end to end: logo, brand identity, brochure, social, the website concept and the 3d exhibition renders; aimed at making a 90-year-old archive feel worth stepping into for a younger audience. my team mates worked alongside me on the concept and helped prototype the site; the brief was a group of four, but the design across every touchpoint here is mine.

brand & logo

a mark that folds open

the logo reworks the library's initials into the pages of a book, wrapped in a wax-seal-style badge, a nod to the archive's history and its unopened-book concept. navy, warm pink and acid yellow-green carry the identity across print and screen, with folded-paper corners used throughout as a recurring motif.

brochure

a tri-fold introduction

a printed leave-behind covering who the library is, what's on, and how to visit, built on the same paper-fold system as the rest of the identity.

website

a homepage that opens up

designed around a ring-bound "book" motif on the homepage, with the library's own events and visiting info pulled through in the new identity.

outdoor booth

bringing the archive outside

a portable exhibition booth for clerkenwell green, modelled, lit and rendered in cinema 4d, with a rotating stand of interchangeable panels so passers-by can flip through library history like pages.

interior banner

a timeline along the wall

a wide timeline banner for the library's reading room, tracing the building from 1738 to today.

social

simple enough to reuse

a set of instagram templates kept deliberately simple, so the library's own team could keep making them after we handed the brand over.

client project halloween horror nights 3d · compositing · motion · live event
the brief

a hell with a thousand doors

the scaremony is the live opening ceremony that kicks off every night of universal studios singapore's halloween horror nights, a month-long, ticketed halloween event. this was a real client project from my time at a studio in singapore, where i was tasked with creating the background visuals for scaremony 11.

the brief called for a hell with multiple doors, built to the client's script and audio. i worked alongside my colleague to bring that vision to life, filming plates on set, then handling most of the compositing, 3d modelling and animation, timed frame-by-frame to the performance.

pro bono client project solo project · social campaign
the brief

a solo campaign, start to finish

playhood is a local singaporean clothing brand i worked with pro bono to create social media marketing content. i handled the whole thing solo, planning the concepts directly with the owner, then filming, modelling, animating and compositing three instagram posts to advertise the brand.

the goal was bigger than three posts, though. the owner dreams of growing playhood's mascots into their own franchise one day, so this was about bringing sunny, kiki and hoodie to life as real characters people could root for, not just repeating the logo. i wanted every video to feel as playful and colourful as the brand itself.

each post pairs tracked live-action footage with 3d work and compositing, built to stand out mid-scroll on instagram.

client project 2d · 3d · motion · live event
about the 90s segment the 2010s segment
about

a decade-hopping christmas show

mel's merry mixtape was a live christmas show performed daily throughout the season at universal studios singapore, taking the audience on a musical trip through the decades. i worked on this as part of a team of three, creating the background visuals that played behind the live performers each afternoon.

my segments were the 90s and 2010s songs, which i took on start to finish: sketches, animatics, 2d and 3d asset creation, modelling, texturing, lighting, compositing and animation, all timed to the music. i also helped with compositing on some of the other songs my teammates led.

the show

live show recording

a recording from a live performance of the full show; asset breakdowns and clean segment cuts of the 90s and 2010s songs coming soon.

the 90s segment

an opulent stage

bright, grand and unmistakably exuberant. i designed the visual world for this segment from the ground up: from modelling each 3d asset, to animating them and compositing the effects. matching the colourful energy of the decade, it was animated to hit every beat of the song live on stage.

the 2010s segment

a pop-up christmas card, come to life

fun and colourful, drawing on paper art for its visual language. every asset was designed and built to look like a layered paper cut-out, so the whole segment reads like a pop-up christmas card brought to life on stage. every scene was built from detailed animatics and synced tightly to the music and the live performers' choreography.
